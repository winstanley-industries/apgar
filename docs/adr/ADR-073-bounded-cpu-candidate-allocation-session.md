# ADR-073: Bounded CPU Candidate-Allocation Session

**Status:** Accepted for P4R-08

**Date:** August 7, 2026

**Applies to:** EPIC-001 P4R-08 deterministic One-World CPU allocation contender

## Context

P4R-06 derives one immutable negotiated-price snapshot and one canonical target
roster from complete immutable P4R-03 candidate pools. P4R-07 executes exactly
that roster through production CPU A*, authenticated candidate construction,
exact admission, one CAN-004 publication transaction, and one refreshed P4R-03
selection/accounting boundary. Neither slice defines how successful epochs are
retained, replay-linked, bounded as a whole, or stopped.

P4R-08 must supply that composition without creating a second price schedule,
route builder, admission path, store authority, selector, or resource
accounting implementation. It must also distinguish a valid bounded allocator
outcome from a fatal coordinator failure: a completed prefix is useful at an
explicit resource stop, but it must not leak when a later association,
publication, arithmetic, or invariant failure occurs.

## Decision

P4R-08 defines `RunCpuCandidateAllocationSession` as one synchronous,
failure-atomic composition of P4R-06 plans and P4R-07 CPU epochs over an
immutable Board IR snapshot, one resource-capacity model, complete owning
source pools, and one retained prepared context per net.

### Authoritative state and canonical inputs

- The coordinator canonicalizes the complete pool and context rosters by Board
  IR `EntityRef` and validates their one-to-one association before planning.
  Caller order, pointer values, container allocation, and repeated execution
  are not semantic.
- Every retained candidate remains an owning `StoredCandidate` handle from
  exact admission. The coordinator never rebuilds an incumbent from its public
  payload, reduces a pool to the selected candidate, or treats CandidateStore
  rank as P4R-03 selection rank.
- After a successful P4R-07 epoch, its complete owned pools become the sole
  source for the next plan and epoch. Earlier pools remain coordinator-local
  immutable values until that epoch is accepted at the session boundary.
- The caller's Board IR, capacity model, owning source handles, compiled
  contexts, and route requests are never mutated.

### Exact composition and snapshot lineage

1. Invoke `PlanNegotiatedRegeneration` on the current complete pools and the
   exact last successfully executed P4R-06 snapshot, or `nullptr` before the
   first epoch.
2. If the plan says `kNoRegenerationRequired`, stop at a fixed point without
   invoking P4R-07. This is the only fixed-point claim and permits a valid
   zero-executed-epoch result.
3. Otherwise reserve the complete next-epoch contribution against checked
   whole-session bounds, then invoke
   `ExecuteCpuTargetedRegenerationEpoch` with those same pools, that exact plan,
   and the exact prior snapshot used to create it.
4. Accept the returned complete pools, refreshed P4R-03 selection/accounting,
   counters, and epoch identity atomically. Only then does the executed plan's
   price snapshot become the prior snapshot for the next planning step.
5. Repeat until the fixed point or a typed bounded stop.

P4R-06 remains the only allocator-price and target authority. P4R-07 remains
the only route/build/admit/publication authority. In particular, the
coordinator does not reuse CS-RR-v1 costs, synthesize or patch a price snapshot,
add or reorder targets, execute a target twice, or add another refreshed
selection/accounting pass.

### Whole-session bounds and typed stops

- The session has positive hard and configured ceilings for executed epochs,
  cumulative source-candidate visits and bytes, targets/route queries, price
  projection visits, reserved CPU work, reserved generated bytes, and atomic
  publication items. All products and accumulations are checked `uint64_t`
  arithmetic. Equality is accepted; the first value above a ceiling stops
  before the next P4R-07 invocation.
- CPU-work and generated-byte reservations use the unchanged configured
  P4R-07 per-query and per-column maxima. The coordinator does not shrink the
  P4R-07 configuration to the remaining session budget because that
  configuration is part of the P4R-07 batch, query, candidate, and epoch
  identities. Actual successful epoch counters must fit their reservations and
  are accumulated separately.
- `kFixedPoint` is a successful terminal reason. `kSessionBoundExhausted`
  retains the last fully accepted pools and selection when the next epoch
  would exceed a composition bound. `kEpochBoundExhausted` retains that same
  state when P4R-06 or P4R-07 reaches one of its own declared bounds. A bounded
  stop records the pending plan when one exists; it never adopts the pending
  snapshot or a failed epoch's pools.
- A bound encountered before any authoritative selection exists is a typed
  session error rather than a partially shaped outcome. Invalid
  configuration/input, association mismatch, arithmetic overflow, host
  resource exhaustion, publication or selection failure, and internal
  invariant failure are fatal. A fatal result exposes no session, completed
  prefix, advanced pool, or advanced price snapshot.
- Precedence at a planning boundary is fatal planner error, fixed point,
  executed-epoch ceiling, then cumulative session reservations. During an
  epoch, P4R-07 precedence remains authoritative. A P4R-07 bound becomes the
  typed epoch stop; any other P4R-07 failure is fatal.

### Replay identities and result

- Every accepted planning step records the P4R-06 plan and snapshot identities,
  prior-snapshot identity, price epoch, input-pool roster identity, and, when
  executed, the P4R-07 batch and epoch identities plus output-pool identity and
  counters. A terminal no-target or pending bounded plan is recorded without
  fabricated execution identities.
- Pool-roster identities bind canonical net order, candidate IDs, payload
  checksums, and logical bytes. The session identity binds associations,
  configuration, initial and final pool identities, the complete step trace,
  terminal reason and bounded diagnostic, final P4R-03 selection/accounting,
  and checked counters.
- These identities are deterministic replay bindings on trusted in-process
  values, not hostile-operator attestations and not serialized evidence.
- Success or a typed bounded stop returns a move-only self-contained value with
  complete owning final pools, final selection/accounting, the optional
  terminal plan, canonical step trace, counters, stop diagnostic, and stable
  identity.

## Independent exact-small reference

Exact-small tests maintain a separate composed state machine that does not call
`RunCpuCandidateAllocationSession`. It invokes P4R-06 and P4R-07 directly,
manually carries only successful P4R-07 owning pools and executed-plan price
snapshots forward, independently accumulates session reservations/counters,
and compares the complete trace, stop, final pool roster, selection/accounting,
and replay identity inputs. Existing P4R-06 and P4R-07 exact-small oracles
remain the independent authorities for price/target derivation and authentic
route/build/admit/publication/selection behavior within each step.

Coverage includes zero-epoch fixed points, multi-epoch progress, typed
epoch/session exhaustion, exact-bound equality and one-under failure, invalid
snapshot chains, pool/context permutations, repeated-run determinism, retained
unselected pool authority, input immutability, and a later fatal error that
must discard every completed prefix.

## Consequences

- P4R-08 delivers one reusable deterministic CPU One-World contender while
  keeping every epoch independently bounded and replayable.
- Complete immutable pools, rather than selected candidates or mutable store
  state, are the only cross-epoch allocator authority.
- Planning the next boundary may recompute P4R-03 through P4R-06, but each
  executed P4R-07 epoch still performs exactly one post-publication refreshed
  P4R-03 selection/accounting. The coordinator adds no selector call of its own.
- A valid bounded stop can be compared or diagnosed without being mislabeled
  as a fatal infrastructure failure. No resume/checkpoint contract is implied;
  introducing one would require a separate identity and lifetime decision.

## Explicit non-goals

This decision does not begin P4R-09, add fixed-pool or interleaved Multi-World
behavior, add GPU allocation, reuse CS-RR-v1 congestion costs, change P4R-03
ranking, share a mutable CandidateStore across epochs, legalize or commit board
geometry, or add serialized evidence schemas, runners, telemetry, readiness,
campaign, operational-capture, publication, or heldout machinery. It does not
port the archived allocation-session implementation or schemas.
